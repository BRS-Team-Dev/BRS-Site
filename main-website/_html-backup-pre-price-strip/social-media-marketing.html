Questions

Social Media, Answered

The things people ask us before they start. If yours is not here, drop us a line.

How much does social media marketing cost?

Management starts at £400 a month for one platform. Content is from £300 a month, and paid ads are from £350 a month plus your ad spend. Most clients pick a plan that bundles the lot, so there is one clear price and no surprises.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X and Pinterest. We start with the two or three where your customers actually spend time rather than spreading you thin across all of them.

Do I need to send you content, or do you make it?

We make it. You get reels, graphics and captions built around your brand each month. If you want to send us photos or clips from the day job we will happily use them, but nothing is expected from you.

How long before I see results?

Paid ads can bring leads in the first couple of weeks. Growing a following the honest way takes longer, and most clients see steady movement over the first 90 days as the content and posting rhythm settle in.

Am I tied into a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month. Give us 30 days notice if you ever want to stop, and you keep everything we have made for you.

Do you report on what is working?

Every month. You get a plain English report showing reach, engagement and, where we run ads, the leads and sales they brought in. No jargon, just what moved and what we are doing next.
