Good To Know

Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before they get in touch.

What does Built Right Studio do?

We're a small UK studio building websites, custom software and CRM systems, brand kits, IT support and social media for small businesses. One team handles the lot.

How fast can you build a website?

Most websites go live in 24 to 72 hours, once we've had our first call and you've approved the direction.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start at £350 for a quick launch and run to around £1,200 for a full build with branding and online store. You get a fixed quote up front, and pay a 50% deposit to book your slot.

Where are you based?

We're UK based. You deal directly with Sean and Bobby, not a call centre or a chain of account managers.

Do you only build websites?

No. Websites are where we started, but we also build custom software and CRMs, run IT support, design brand kits and manage social media.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We offer monthly care plans for websites and monthly IT support, so once you're live you're not on your own.
