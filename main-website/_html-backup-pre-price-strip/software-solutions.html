Questions

Software questions, answered

What people usually want to know before they start a trial.

What is the six system platform?

It's one piece of software that covers six jobs most businesses run separately: CRM, HR, tasks, recruitment, support and operations. Everything sits behind a single login instead of six disconnected tools.

How much does the software cost?

Plans start at £99 a month for small teams and are priced by team size, not per seat. Every system is included in every plan, and you can move up or down whenever your team changes.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get a full month free with no card required. Billing only starts once you decide to roll it out to your team.

Do you also build custom software?

Yes. Most teams are covered by the subscription, but when your requirements are genuinely one of a kind we build a platform from scratch, owned entirely by you and priced to the project.

Can I move my existing data across?

Yes. We help migrate your existing data so you're not starting from a blank system, and there's no need to rip out what already works.

Do I have to use all six systems?

No. They all come included, but you use the ones you need. Most teams start with the CRM and switch the others on as they grow.

Prefer to Talk First?

If your needs are bigger than a quick signup, like multiple integrations, custom modules or a migration from another platform, book a free consultation and we'll design something that fits.
