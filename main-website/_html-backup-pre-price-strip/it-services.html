Questions

IT support questions, answered

The things people ask us most before they get started.

How much does IT support cost?

Managed IT support starts at £40 a month for small teams, with fixed monthly pricing and no setup fee. Most small businesses settle on the £89/month Growth plan. You can move up or down a plan whenever your team changes size.

How fast do you respond?

Under an hour on average during business hours. Urgent problems jump the queue, and the Premium plan includes 24/7 cover for when something breaks at 2am.

Are you based in the UK?

Yes. You'll talk to a UK based team that speaks plain English, not a scripted overseas call centre.

Do I need to sign a long contract?

No. Plans are month to month. You stay because it's working, not because you're locked in.

Do you support Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Both. We set them up, move your old email across with no downtime, and manage them day to day.

What if I already have some IT in place?

That's fine. We pick up what you've got, fix what's shaky, and fill the gaps. There's no need to rip anything out.

Do you handle cyber security?

Yes. Security patching, backups, and spam and phishing protection come as standard, with security audits on the higher plans.
