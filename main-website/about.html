About Us

The Team Behind Built Right

We're Sean and Bobby, a two person studio in the UK. We build the websites, software and IT that small businesses need to look sharp and run smoothly, and we do it quickly, without the agency price tag.

Our Story

How It All Started

Sean

Sales & Creative

Bobby

Developer & Tech

"Sean sells it. Bobby builds it.
Clients get it quick."

We met through a shared frustration: why does getting a quality website take so long and cost so much?

Sean had years of experience in sales and creative direction, helping businesses understand what they needed but watching them wait months for delivery. Bobby was building websites as a developer, seeing projects drag on due to miscommunication and scope creep.

So we teamed up. Sean looks after the client side: working out what you actually need, sketching the plan, and keeping the quality bar high. Bobby takes that and builds it, with code that's clean and quick.

The result is a process with no bloat. Most websites go live in 24 to 72 hours instead of weeks, and there are no drawn out meetings or eye watering agency invoices along the way.

That's the whole idea behind Built Right Studio. Do good work, do it fast, and charge a fair price for it.

Good To Know

Common questions

A few things people usually want to check before they get in touch.

What does Built Right Studio do?

We're a small UK studio that builds websites, custom software and CRM systems, brand kits, and handles IT support and social media for small businesses. If it's digital and your business needs it, there's a good chance we do it.

Where are you based?

We're a UK based team. Everything's handled by Sean and Bobby directly, so you're never passed around a call centre or an account manager.

How fast can you deliver?

Most websites go live in 24 to 72 hours. Bigger software builds take longer, but we'll always give you a straight timeline before you commit to anything.

Do you only build websites?

No. Websites are where we started, but we also build custom software and CRMs, run IT support, design brand kits and manage social media. One team for the lot.

How much does it cost?

Everything is priced up front with no hidden fees, so you'll always know the number before we start.
