Questions

Website questions, answered

The things people ask us most before getting started.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites go live in 24 to 72 hours once we've had our first call and you've signed off the direction. Larger sites take a little longer, and we give you a clear timeline before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Every website is quoted up front based on what you need. You get a fixed quote up front, with no hidden fees.

Do I need to provide the content and images?

If you've got text and photos, great, we'll use them. If not, we can work from what you give us and help write and source the rest. You won't be left staring at a blank page.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site we build works properly on phones, tablets and desktops. Over half of web traffic is on mobile, so it's never an afterthought.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Our refresh service keeps your content and domain while we modernise the design, speed it up and fix the mobile experience. It's a new site without starting from scratch.

Do I own the website when it's finished?

Yes. Once the balance is paid, the website and its files are yours. No catch, and you're free to host it wherever you like.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly care plans that handle security, backups, updates and small changes, so your site stays fast and secure after it goes live.
