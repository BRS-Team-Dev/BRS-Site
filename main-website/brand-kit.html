Questions

Branding, Answered

The things people ask us before they start. If yours is not here, drop us a line.

How much does a logo and brand kit cost?

We quote every project up front based on what you need, so there are no surprises once we get going.

What is included in the Brand Kit?

The Brand Kit gives you a full logo suite, a colour palette, font pairings, a brand guidelines document and templates for your business card and social profiles. It is everything you need to look consistent wherever people find you.

How long does the branding process take?

Most logo projects are wrapped up in one to two weeks. A full brand kit usually takes two to three weeks, depending on how quickly you send feedback and how many rounds of changes we go through. We will give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do I own the logo and files when it is finished?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the logo and all the files are yours to keep and use however you like. You get full ownership and usage rights, and we hand over the editable source files too, so you are never locked in.

How many rounds of revisions do I get?

Logo design includes two rounds of refinements so we can shape the strongest concept into the final mark. Our Premium package comes with unlimited revisions if you would rather take your time and get every detail exactly right.

What file formats will I receive?

You get scalable vector files (SVG and AI) so your logo stays crisp at any size, plus PNG and JPG exports for everyday use, and favicon and app icon versions. Everything is supplied in full colour, mono and reversed variants ready for screen and print.
